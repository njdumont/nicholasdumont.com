## About

I work in power markets and care about how complex systems behave under
stress, how to price risk, and how to build skills that stay useful over
decades instead of a single job.

My background is in finance and mathematics. Day to day, I sit close to the
grid and real-time prices; in the background, I'm building a deeper toolkit
in probability, statistics, and optimization so I can reason more clearly
about uncertainty and leverage.

This site exists primarily as a place for me to keep a public record of what
I’m interested in and working on. If you’re here for any reason—hiring,
collaboration, or curiosity—you’re seeing the parts I’m comfortable putting
on the open internet, not the full story.

## What tends to hold my attention

- The link between physical constraints (generation, transmission, fuel) and market outcomes.
- Quantitative decision-making: estimation, risk, and model failure modes.
- Long-lived projects that compound (skills, relationships, capital).
- Self-directed education as an ongoing system, not a one-time credential.
