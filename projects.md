## Current projects

- **Energy-trading mental models**<br>
  Writing down and stress-testing the mental models I use in real-time power
  trading: which drivers actually move prices, how constraints show up, and
  how different markets (day-ahead, real-time, neighboring RTOs) interact.
- **Personal quantitative curriculum**<br>
  A structured path through probability, statistics, optimization, and
  financial engineering. The goal is not to collect courses, but to be able
  to pose and solve better questions about markets and risk.
- **Knowledge map for books and concepts**<br>
  A lightweight system to map books, key ideas, and projects into a graph,
  so it’s easier to see connections and avoid re-learning the same things
  in slightly different language.
- **Training and performance framework**<br>
  Strength and conditioning geared toward long-term work capacity and
  resilience, not just aesthetics. This shows up here because it directly
  affects how well I can do the rest.
- **This site**<br>
  Minimal static website to make it easy to publish small updates about what
  I’m working on, without a big platform or CMS in the way.
